▶ intro2se-w2-sdlc.fig · Requirements
FROM WISH TO TESTABLE CONTRACT

What does “a good study plan” mean?

VAGUE

“Generate a good study plan.”

Good for whom? Which constraints? How fast? How verified?

TESTABLE

“Generate a seven-day plan within five seconds.”

No class overlap · no deadline omission · no excess over declared study hours.

▶ appendix.fig · Waterfall detail
APPENDIX · PREDICTIVE MODEL

Waterfall: major phases and formal gates

Useful when

Constraints are stable, evidence is formal, and transitions require approval.

Main risk

Wrong assumptions can survive until working software and user feedback arrive late.

▶ appendix.fig · Incremental detail
APPENDIX · ADAPTIVE DELIVERY

Incremental: repeat the work, grow the product

Inside each increment

Specify · build · validate

Do enough of all essential work to create a usable result.

Advantage

Evidence arrives early

Users react to working capability before the entire scope is complete.

Control needed

Protect architecture

Repeated short-term changes can degrade structure without refactoring and governance.

▶ appendix.fig · Other models
APPENDIX · TWO MORE PLAN-DRIVEN SHAPES

V-Model & Spiral

V-Model — every build phase is paired with a test phase
